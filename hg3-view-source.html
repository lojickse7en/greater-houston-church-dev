    About GH³

    Our Mission

    To offer opportunities for our church to love and serve our communities that reflect the heart of God. We want to highlight and communicate to you the ways we are serving across regions.

    Our Vision

    Every member has opportunities to serve using their gifts. This can help us to build relationships and to strengthen connections within our communities.
